Cool Theme Auto-Sync

Install the companion extension to magically sync your logins and cookies to the Cloud Browser without typing any passwords.

Download cool-theme.zip

Installation Guide

  1. Download & Extract the zip file above to a folder on your computer.
  2. Open Chrome or Brave and go to chrome://extensions in your URL bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (toggle in the top right corner).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.

Once installed, the OneDesk Cloud Browser will automatically sync your active session cookies!
