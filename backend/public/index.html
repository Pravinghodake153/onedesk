Documentation

Everything you need to get started.

1. Initial Setup

Install the downloaded application on the device you wish to control. Make sure it is connected to the internet.

2. Granting Permissions

For OneDesk to function properly, you must grant accessibility permissions (on Android) or Screen Recording permissions (on Mac/Windows). This allows OneDesk to capture the screen and control inputs.

Tip: Android users should enable "Ignore Battery Optimizations" to allow unattended access while the phone sleeps.

3. Connecting via Web App

Once the host app is running, navigate to the Web App from any browser. You will see your device listed under "Available Computers". Click to instantly connect!

Troubleshooting

Having issues? Here are common fixes.

Q: My phone is not showing up online!

A: Ensure your phone has an active internet connection. If you are using a local server, ensure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.

Q: I click connect, but the screen is black.

A: This means Screen Recording permission was denied. On Android, force close the app, open it again, and click "Grant Remote Access". On Mac, check System Settings > Privacy > Screen Recording.

Q: The connection drops when my phone sleeps.

A: Your phone's battery optimizer is killing the app. Go to Android Settings > Apps > OneDesk > Battery, and set it to "Unrestricted" or "Ignore Battery Optimizations".

Q: Microphone toggle isn't working.

A: Ensure you granted "Record Audio" permissions when the app first launched.
